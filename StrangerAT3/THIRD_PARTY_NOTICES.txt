PYTHON (bundled runtime)
------------------------
Folder:   StrangerAT3\python\
What:     The CPython 3.14 interpreter and standard library, used to run the
          tools in ModTools\. It is private to AT3: it does not install
          anything, does not touch the Windows registry or PATH, and does not
          affect any other Python on your computer. Deleting AT3 removes it.

          https://www.python.org/ by StrangerAT3\build_runtime.py, laid out

---------------------------------------

Stranger's Wrath HD, made by a separate developer. SWSE is NOT included with
AT3 and is distributed separately under its own terms. AT3 requires it.
